# ImageSplat Studio v0.1.2 — Cài đặt Windows (OFFLINE)

## Không cần cài Python!

Ban nay da dong goi san Python + thu vien trong app.

## Cach cai (2 buoc)

### 1. Giai nen
- Tai file: **ImageSplatStudio-0.1.2-win-offline.zip** (~250 MB)
- Click phai → **Extract All...**
- Chon thu muc: `C:\ImageSplatStudio\`

### 2. Chay app
- Double-click **`ImageSplat Studio.exe`**
- Hoac chay **`CaiDat.bat`** (neu can)
